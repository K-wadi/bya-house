# BYA House brand kit

## 1. Brand name
BYA House

## 2. Slogan
Covered in grace.

## 3. Positioning
A modest ready-to-wear label for women who want coverage without giving up cut, colour, or presence. Sits between high-street modest fashion and couture abaya houses: tailored co-ords, belted shirt-dresses, and tiered occasion dresses at accessible premium prices.

## 4. Target audience
Women 22 to 40 who dress modestly by choice: working professionals, students, and guests at weddings and Eid gatherings. They shop on Instagram, care about fabric and fit, and want one wardrobe that moves from office to dinner to celebration.

## 5. Brand personality
Feminine, romantic, composed, refined, warm, quietly confident, unhurried.
BYA House speaks like a trusted stylist: soft voice, sure eye, never loud.

## 7. Typography
- Headings: **Bodoni Moda** (Google Fonts), weights 400 and 500. High-contrast didone, matches the "BYA" wordmark.
- Body and labels: **Jost** (Google Fonts), weights 300 and 400. Labels set in caps with wide tracking (0.3em), matching "HOUSE".

## 8. Logo direction
Use the existing logo: a single-line drawing of a woman seen from behind in a draped gown with a bow sash, over soft blush, sage, and sand washes, a gold crescent and gold dust. Wordmark "BYA" in a didone serif with a blush half-moon tucked into the B, "HOUSE" in thin wide-tracked caps beneath, closed by a short gold rule. On the dark site: wordmark in blush white, rule in gold. Favicon: a gold crescent with a "B".

## 9. Product description
Modest ready-to-wear, sold as collections:
- **Tailored co-ords**: belted blazer with wide-leg trouser in textured linen-blend (chocolate, charcoal pinstripe).
- **Easy sets**: oversized button shirt with matching wide-leg trouser in crinkle crepe (butter, blush, cobalt).
- **Shirt-dresses and abayas**: full-length, belted, A-line skirt, cuffed sleeves; raw indigo denim and soft twill.
- **Occasion dresses**: tiered chiffon and floral jacquard with bishop sleeves and a tie waist (black, sea glass, rose); pleated mousseline with a button placket and wide waistband (plum, emerald).
- Every piece: ankle or full length, long sleeves, high or band collar, opaque or lined fabrics, relaxed fit through the body.
- Sizes XS to 3XL. Ships in a blush tissue box.
- Prices (placeholders): easy sets from €89, shirt-dresses from €119, occasion dresses from €129, tailored co-ords from €149.

## 10. Key benefits
- Full coverage, cut to flatter: long lines, defined waists, wide legs.
- Fabrics that breathe and drape: crepe, linen-blend, chiffon over lining.
- Matching sets that work apart: one co-ord, three outfits.
- Office to Eid in one wardrobe: tailoring for weekdays, tiers for celebrations.
- Lined where it matters, so nothing is sheer.
- Sizes XS to 3XL with free exchanges.

## 11. Visual mood
Low warm light in a quiet baroque salon at dusk: plaster walls, gilded oval mirrors, soft folds of chiffon catching gold rim light. Floating gold dust motes drifting slowly through the light, like the dots in the logo. Fabric in slow motion, a hem settling, a sash lifting. Camera: slow, steady glides, shallow depth of field, never handheld.

## 17. Design bans
- No cream or off-white page background.
- No italic accent words inside headlines.
- No numbered "01 / 02 / 03" section labels.
- No monospace eyebrow labels.
- No pill-shaped buttons.
- No gradient-filled headline text.
- No emoji as icons.
- No three identical feature cards in a row.
- No purple-to-blue gradients.
- No glass effect on every card.
- No copy over the figure: all beat copy sits on the film's calm left side.

## 18. Experience
Option B: cinematic film + images. The five beats in section 15 are the film's visual journey.

**Film brief:** one continuous 14s shot, no cuts. Starts from the hero still: the woman in the plum pleated chiffon maxi walks slowly away through the dim salon toward the gilded oval mirror. The camera follows with a slow forward push. Pleats sway, gold dust drifts in the beam. Low-key light, gold rim light on shoulders and pleats. Left side stays calm in shadow for headlines. Final frame at rest: she has stopped three-quarter away before the mirror, hem settling on the floor, calm space on the right.
